Pub Games – Delete your account

In the app

  1. Open Pub Games and go to Account.
  2. Tap Delete account, check what will be deleted, and type DELETE to confirm.

Without the app

Email geraatsdeveloping@gmail.com from the Google account you sign in with, with the subject "Delete my Pub Games account". We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

Your account, profile, nickname, profile picture, player code, stats, purchases, feedback, player links and blocks. In games you played with others, only your own result is removed.

What is kept

Games you added to the shared game library stay available to other players, without your name. Inbox messages and review requests you sent to the moderators can be kept; ask by email to have them removed too. Google Play keeps its own record of your purchases. A running Pub Games Pass is billed by Google Play: cancel it there first.
