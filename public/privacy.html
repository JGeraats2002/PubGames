Pub Games – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Pub Games ("the app") is an app with pub and party games, including drinking games, for adults (18+), made by an independent developer in the Netherlands. This policy explains what data the app uses, why, and how you can delete it.

Who is responsible

Pub Games, contact: geraatsdeveloping@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account: when you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address and a user id from Google (via Firebase Authentication). We never see your Google password.
  • Profile: the name, nickname and optional profile picture you choose, and your player code. Players you link with can see your name, nickname and picture.
  • Game results: which games you played, when, and whether you won, lost or tied, so the app can show statistics to you and the players you played with.
  • Player links and blocks: which players you linked, unlinked or blocked.
  • Feedback and messages: feedback you send (with your name and email so we can reply) and messages in the in-app inbox.
  • Pictures: pictures you add to games or your profile, from your gallery, the camera or the drawing tool. The camera is only used when you choose to take a picture.
  • Purchases: which games or Pub Games Pass you bought, and the purchase token from Google Play to check the purchase. We never receive your payment details.

Players and games you create are also stored on your phone. The app does not use advertising, analytics or tracking, and does not use your location.

Why we use it

Only to run the app: signing you in, syncing your games and stats, letting you play with linked players, checking purchases, and answering your feedback. Legal basis: performing the service you asked for (GDPR art. 6(1)(b)).

Where it is stored and who receives it

Data is stored in Google Firebase (Firestore and Cloud Functions) in the European Union. Purchases are handled by Google Play. Google acts as our processor. We do not sell or share your data with anyone else. Data is sent over encrypted (https) connections.

How long we keep it

As long as you have an account. When you delete your account, your data is deleted (see below).

Deleting your account and data

In the app: Account → Delete account. This deletes your profile, player code, picture, stats, purchases, feedback, links and blocks right away. In games you played with others, only your own result is removed. Games you added to the shared library stay, without your name. You can also ask for deletion by email: see Delete your account.

A running Pub Games Pass is billed by Google Play: cancel it in Google Play first.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data, and you can complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens). Email us and we answer within 30 days.

Age

The app is only for people of 18 years and older and asks you to confirm this.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date.
